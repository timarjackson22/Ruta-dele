# Reading & Listening: headline pane and control bar brief

**Problems (screenshot, a saved Copa Airlines article):** the top of the page is a wall of admin before the text starts (level picker, "English translation: complete", "Questions: vocabulary in context", Write questions, Clean up text with its explanation, Rename or delete), the headline is plain, it scrolls away while the text is read aloud, and the player bar is squeezed since "Cues" became "Verbatim" (the speed shows as "0.9›", the ••• button takes a full slot).

**Headline pane (Reading and Listening, built-in and your own texts)**
- One designed card at the top: a coloured band in the text type's colour (story, news, podcast, song, reading, your text) with its icon, chips for type · level · minutes (and country when known), the headline, and one quiet line under it (subtitle or source · words).
- A small **Options ▾** button in the card holds everything else: for your own texts the level, translation, questions, clean-up and **Rename or delete**; for songs the note about the rhythm. Built-in texts without extras show no button. The first-visit hint line goes.
- The podcast "listen first" note stays (it explains the hidden text).

**Frozen while reading**
- When reading aloud starts, the card sticks under the top bar and shrinks to a compact strip (chips + headline on one or two lines + a thin progress bar showing how far the reading has got). The body keeps gliding up beneath it; each line fades out as it slides under the card. Pausing or stopping lets the card scroll normally again and grow back.

**Control bar**
- The ••• slot becomes a narrow **▾ drop-down** at the end of the bar. It opens a small panel above the bar with Speed, Hide text, English and (for songs) Rhythm, instead of wrapping the bar onto a second row.
- The bar keeps: back · play · forward · position · Verbatim · ▾ — with room to breathe on a phone; the speed is no longer squeezed.

**Done when** the pane and options are tested for built-in and saved texts (rename/delete still work), the card freezes and compacts while reading and lets go when paused, lines fade under it, the drop-down opens/closes with all its controls working, nothing overflows on small phones, and earlier tests pass.
