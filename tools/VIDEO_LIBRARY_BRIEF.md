# Video library brief — real Spanish, short clips, every kind of listening

**Goal:** a library of short videos (4 minutes or less each) of native speakers in real situations, sorted by type and level, and the option to remove any video from your list. No paid services.

**Categories (at least 4 videos each, mixed levels A1–C1)**
1. Everyday dialogues — ordering, shopping, directions, small talk between native speakers.
2. Interviews — street interviews and one-to-one conversations.
3. News — short news reports from Spanish-language broadcasters.
4. Weather — forecasts as presented on TV or by weather services.
5. Sports — match reports, commentary, post-match interviews.
6. Music — popular songs' official videos (catch words and retell only: the app never shows lyrics).
7. Podcasts — excerpts from Spanish podcasts for learners and natives.

**Sources and rules**
- YouTube videos found by searching the web; only IDs seen in search results are used (none invented).
- Each item gets a level (A1–C1), a one-line note on what it's about, and the channel name.
- 4-minute cap: every item plays as a clip with a start and end time (YouTube's player supports this), so a long podcast or report plays only the chosen 4 minutes or less, and the length is shown on the card.
- If YouTube won't play an item inside the app, the app marks it, moves it out of the main list ("not playable in the app" with Open in YouTube) so the list stays clean.

**The list**
- Filter chips by category and by level; each card shows the category, level and clip length.
- "Remove" on every video (cards in edit mode and on the video page). Your own videos are deleted; library videos are hidden, with Undo and a "Show removed videos" option to bring them back.

**Exercises** stay the same for every video (catch words, say it, retell, Ask Profe); points when the clip has been watched.

**Done when** each category has at least 4 items, the filters, the clip limits, removing/restoring and auto-hiding are covered by automated tests against a simulated player, and the earlier tests pass. Whether each real video plays inside the app is checked on the phone (YouTube can't be reached from the test environment).
