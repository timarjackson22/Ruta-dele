## Profe: root causes found and fixed

| # | Defect (severity) | Root cause | Fix | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | "Ok Profe" never heard while a lesson is read aloud (High) | The wake listener is turned off whenever any speech plays (to avoid Profe hearing itself), and read-aloud counts as speech, so Profe was deaf for the whole reading | With the offline (Vosk) listener, which reads the mic directly, takes no audio focus and only knows the wake phrase, listening continues during read-aloud (not during Profe's own replies). A line that itself says "profe" is ignored. With the phone's recogniser (may take audio focus) the old rule stays and the pill says "Reading · tap to talk" | sMIC: 20/20 samples listening during reading |
| P2 | Interrupting read-aloud left it stuck: silent, button still "Pause" (High) | Any other speech or mic start cancels the TTS; Android reports no "interrupted" event, so read-aloud waited for ever | One guard on the speech engine: an outside speak/cancel pauses read-aloud properly (button shows Play). Tap-to-talk now pauses and resumes it like the wake phrase | sMIC 4–5 |
| P3 | "Ok Profe, pausa" during reading answered with a video search (Medium) | The wake had already paused the reading, so "pausa" no longer matched "pause" (only valid while playing) and fell through to the search | Media held by "Ok Profe" still counts as playing for understanding; "pause" then keeps it paused | sMIC 2 |
| P4 | Read-aloud started into Profe's open follow-up mic / follow-up mic paused resumed reading (Medium) | Two owners of the audio with no rule between them | Reading starting closes Profe's follow-up mic; if reading has resumed, Profe doesn't reopen the mic | sMIC 3, overlap check |
| P5 | Commands missing or wrong in lessons (Medium) | Not in the registry; "previous page" meant "previous screen", "go to the start" meant Home | New: next/previous page, start/end of lesson, page/scroll view, read the full lesson, read that line again; wording added for "explain this grammar rule", "start a speaking exercise", "open a practice exam", "open a lesson" | sMIC: 26/26 commands executed (English and Spanish) |
| P6 | Diagnostics too short to root-cause on a phone (Low) | 40-line log, no timings | 200 events; each listening turn logs mic-open latency and turn length; read-aloud pauses are logged. No audio and no conversation text is stored beyond the command words | Mic diagnostics |

Turn-taking scenarios covered by tests (sMIC, sH): mic before Profe speaks, interrupting Profe, speaking during read-aloud,
Profe finishing then listening, repeated taps (one mic session), permission denied, app in background, lesson switch while listening,
no speech into an open mic, never two voices at once.
