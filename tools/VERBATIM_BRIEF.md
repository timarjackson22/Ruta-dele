# Verbatim brief — one name for the live word-by-word translation

**Goal:** the pop-up English that follows the Spanish word by word (or phrase by phrase) as it's read or spoken is called **Verbatim** everywhere you can see it.

**Where it appears**
- Reading and Listening player bar: the switch button reads "Verbatim" (was "Cues"); its tooltip and screen-reader label say "Verbatim: live word-by-word translation".
- The hint under a text: "Verbatim translates word by word as the voice reads" (was "cue tips follow the voice word by word").
- Settings: "Verbatim" with "live word-by-word translation in Reading, Listening and Videos" (was "Translation cue tips").
- Videos caption window: the "Verbatim" button (was "Cue tips").
- One setting behind all of them: turning Verbatim off in one place turns it off everywhere.

**Done when** no visible "cue tip(s)" or "Cues" label is left, the buttons fit on a phone, and the earlier tests pass.
