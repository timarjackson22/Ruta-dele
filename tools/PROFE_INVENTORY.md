# Profe feature-and-action inventory

Generated by `tools/profe_inventory.py` from `tools/profe_registry.py` on 2026-11-15. Do not edit by hand.

**Sources of truth:** voice actions and their patterns live in `tools/profe_registry.py` (built into `index.html` as `pv-registry`); handlers in `tools/pvoice.js` (`PVH`); every route in `route()` and every `data-act` button must have a status here, which `node tools/profe_check.js` enforces in CI.

**Status words:** *complete* = a voice command runs it and checks the outcome; *pending* = reachable by touch, voice planned (reason given); *unsuitable* = not a sensible voice action (reason given). Any button with a visible label can also be pressed by naming it ("tap Music") through `find.open`, which checks that something changed before reporting success.

## Content Profe can find by name (`find.open`, built at runtime by `pvIndex()` in tools/pvoice.js)

Screens · levels · every unit (Spanish and English titles) · level flashcards and exams · every listening and reading text (songs, podcasts, stories, poems, news, history, culture, readings) and your own saved texts · every video with its singers/channel · grammar guides · grammar pages · writing tasks · the buttons, filters and links visible on the current screen. Spanish titles also answer to their English words through the app's dictionary ("the market" → «En el mercado»). Clear matches run at once; otherwise Profe offers the closest three and you answer with the number.

## Not reachable by voice (known gaps)

- Individual dictionary word pages and individual verbs by name (Profe answers meaning and conjugation questions instead).
- Answering inside exam papers and the quick drill by voice (answers by touch; the speaking paper uses the microphone).
- Anything that needs typing long text (essays) — dictation into the writing box is not wired to Profe.
