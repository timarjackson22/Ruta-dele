RUTA DELE: INSTALL IT AS AN APP
================================

STEP 1. Put it online (once, about 5 minutes, easiest on a computer)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Unzip ruta-dele-app.zip. You get a folder called "ruta-dele-app".
2. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
3. Drag the whole "ruta-dele-app" folder onto the page.
4. Sign up for a free Netlify account when asked, so your site stays online.
5. Netlify gives you a link like https://something-random.netlify.app
   Optional: Site configuration > Change site name, e.g. ruta-dele-tunombre.netlify.app

STEP 2. Install it on your phone
--------------------------------
Android (Chrome):
  1. Open your Netlify link in Chrome.
  2. Tap "Install app" when it appears, or tap the menu (three dots) > "Install app".
  3. Ruta DELE now sits in your app drawer like any other app.

iPhone (Safari; it must be Safari):
  1. Open your Netlify link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen", then "Add".
  4. Open it from the new icon. It runs full screen, without browser bars.

Open it once while online. After that it also works offline. Audio uses your
phone's built-in Spanish voices; on some Android phones these need a
connection the first time.

UPDATING
--------
When you get a new version of the folder, drag it onto your site's
"Deploys" page in Netlify. The app updates the next time you open it online.
Your progress stays on your phone.
